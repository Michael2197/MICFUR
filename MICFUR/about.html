Furniture studio since 2021

Home / About

A small studio with a narrow focus.

We design only a handful of pieces at a time so we can obsess over posture, material feel, and how a chair actually lives in a real home.

Calibrated comfort

Tested with people who sit for long stretches of time, not just styled for photos.

Honest materials

Finishes selected for how they age and feel in the hand, not just the spec sheet.

Transparent process

We share how each piece is made, from prototyping to the workshop that builds it.

Long‑term thinking

Replaceable parts and timeless silhouettes designed to outlast fast furniture cycles.

The people behind the chairs.

Small, cross‑disciplinary, and close to every decision from sketch to shipping label.

Lawson Arnold

Creative director

Sets the tone for each collection and keeps every piece aligned with the studios calm, useful aesthetic.

Jeremy Walker

Product engineer

Translates sketches into durable frames, joints, and mechanisms that feel solid for years.

Patrik White

Materials lead

Works directly with suppliers to source fabrics, foams, and finishes that age gracefully.

Kathryn Ryan

Customer experience

Helps customers find the right piece for their space and stays close to feedback after delivery.
